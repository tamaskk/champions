# Store listing (App Store / Google Play)

## Name
Spinvincible – Football Draft XI

## Subtitle (App Store, max 30)

## Short description (Google Play, max 80)
Spin eras, leagues and clubs, draft a legendary XI and simulate a perfect season.

## Description
Spin a formation, then spin a decade, a league and a club – and draft one player from that real
squad. Fill all eleven spots, build chemistry between team-mates and compatriots, and take your XI
into a simulated league season, a Champions League run, or a match against one of the 40 greatest
club seasons ever.

- Top five European leagues, 1960s to today
- Daily Challenge: the same rules and the same spins for everyone, one attempt a day
- Legends: beat Sacchi's Milan, the Invincibles, the Wembley Barça… and collect them
- Head-to-head against a random player
- Global leaderboard of the highest-rated XIs
- Share your XI as an image or a link – can your friends beat it?
